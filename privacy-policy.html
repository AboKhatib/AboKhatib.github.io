The accessed data is used only to execute automation workflows configured by the account owner. ## How Data Is Used Google data may be used to: * Read and update Google Sheets. * Read or manage files in Google Drive. * Send or process emails through Gmail. * Read or manage calendar events through Google Calendar. The data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. ## Data Sharing Google account data is not sold or shared with third parties except when necessary to operate an explicitly configured automation or service. ## Data Storage Authentication credentials and tokens are stored within the owner's self-hosted n8n instance and are used only for the configured automation workflows. ## Data Retention Data is retained only as long as necessary for the configured automation workflows. The account owner can revoke Google's authorization at any time through their Google Account settings. ## Contact For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact: [moh477677@gmail.com](mailto:moh477677@gmail.com)
